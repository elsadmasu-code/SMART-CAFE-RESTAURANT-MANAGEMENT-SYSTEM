**Languages:** English & Amharic (አማርኛ)

## 1. Customer User Guide / የደንበኛ መመሪያ

### 1.1 Accessing the Menu / ሜኑውን ማግኘት
Customers can view the Fkr Cafe menu without needing to download an app or register an account:
1. **Direct Website:** Open your phone's browser and type `www.fkrcafe.com`.
2. **Google Search:** Type `Fkr Cafe Kombolcha` in Google Search and tap the **Website / Menu** button.
3. **Table QR Code:** Aim your camera at the QR code on your dining table. Your table number will be automatically attached to your order.
4. **Android App:** Download the official "Fkr Cafe" application from Google Play.

*በአማርኛ:*
1. በቀጥታ በስልክዎ ብሮውዘር `www.fkrcafe.com` ብለው ይግቡ።
2. ጎግል (Google) ላይ `Fkr Cafe Kombolcha` ብለው በመፈለግ የድር ጣቢያ ሊንኩን ይጫኑ።
3. ጠረጴዛዎ ላይ ያለውን የQR ኮድ በስልክዎ ካሜራ ስካን ያድርጉ።

### 1.2 Placing an Order / ትዕዛዝ መስጠት
1. Select your preferred language at the top right (**English** or **አማርኛ**).
2. Browse categories: Coffee, Fast Food, Traditional Ethiopian, Fresh Juices, Pastries.
3. Tap on any dish (e.g., *Special Beef Tibs* / *ልዩ የበሬ ጥብስ*).
4. Choose optional variants (Spicy level, extra cheese, cup size).
5. Tap **Add to Cart / ወደ ቅርጫት ጨምር**.
6. Review your cart and select **Dine-In (ጠረጴዛ ላይ)**, **Takeaway (ይዞ መሄድ)**, or **Delivery (ማድረስ)**.
7. Choose your payment method: **Cash (በጥሬ ገንዘብ)**, **Telebirr (ቴሌብር)**, or **CBE Birr (ሲቢኢ ብር)**.
8. Tap **Place Order / እዘዝ** to receive your live tracking ticket.

### 1.3 Calling the Waiter / አስተናጋጅ መጥራት
If you need water, extra napkins, the bill, or assistance, tap the floating **Call Waiter (አስተናጋጅ ጥራ)** button on your screen. The floor waiter will receive an immediate notification on their device with your table number.

## 2. Cashier Operations Guide / የካሺየር መመሪያ

1. Log in to the management system using your cashier account.
2. At the start of your shift, click **Open Register (ካሽ መመዝገቢያ ክፈት)** and enter the initial petty cash float (e.g., 500 ETB).
3. The **Incoming Orders** screen shows submitted customer orders in real time.
4. When a customer pays:
   - **Cash:** Collect the amount, enter cash received, and click **Confirm Payment**.
   - **Telebirr / CBE Birr:** Ask the customer for their transaction SMS or transaction ID. Verify the reference and amount in your merchant app/terminal, then enter the reference and click **Verify & Confirm**.
5. Once confirmed, the order automatically dispatches to the kitchen screen (KDS) and a fiscal receipt prints.
6. At shift completion, click **Close Register**, enter final counted cash, and review discrepancies.

---

## 3. Kitchen Display System (KDS) Guide / የወጥ ቤት ሰራተኞች መመሪያ

1. Mount the kitchen display tablet/monitor in the food preparation area.
2. New orders appear automatically with an audible chime:
   - Green border: Fresh ticket ($<10$ mins).
   - Yellow border: In preparation ($10-20$ mins).
   - Red flashing: Urgent / delayed ($>20$ mins).
3. Tap **Accept (ተቀበል)** when beginning preparation.
4. Tap **Ready (ተጠናቋል)** when the plate is ready for pickup. The waiter assigned to that table is immediately notified.

---

## 4. Waiter Operations Guide / የአስተናጋጆች መመሪያ

1. Log in to your waiter account on your smartphone or handheld terminal.
2. When kitchen staff marks a dish **Ready**, your device will vibrate and display the table number and order contents.
3. Collect the tray from the kitchen counter and deliver to the table.
4. Tap **Mark Delivered (ደርሷል)** on your screen.
5. Service requests (e.g., *Table 4: Bring Bill*) will appear as priority notification cards.

---

## 5. Storekeeper & Inventory Guide / የእቃ ግምጃ ቤት መመሪያ

1. Track ingredients (Flour, Cooking Oil, Coffee Beans, Meat, Milk, Sugar, Tomatoes) in real time.
2. As orders are prepared, raw ingredient stock is automatically deducted according to the registered recipe formula.
3. Review the **Low Stock Alerts (ያለቀባቸው እቃዎች)** widget daily.
4. When new supplies arrive from vendors, click **Record Purchase (ግዢ መዝግብ)**, select the supplier, enter unit cost and quantity received.
5. If items spoil or break, log them under **Record Wastage (ብልሽት መዝግብ)** with the exact reason code.

## 6. General Manager & Business Owner Guide / ስራ አስኪያጅ እና ባለቤት መመሪያ
